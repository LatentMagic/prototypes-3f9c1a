Game Unbound: marks and wordmarks, round 3

Game Unbound is about what happens when an agent runs the game: play that isn't fixed in advance and goes wherever you take it. Round 3 tries to say that, in the system's whole set of shapes and cover colours rather than only today's green and orange.

  • Two or three bold masses, the way M0 is built. No thin points, no hairline gaps: each mark has to read from across a room and as a 16px favicon.
  • Each mark carries one idea about the name (released, opened, played by an agent), stated on its card.
  • Every mark has colourways from the cover palette. Tap one to use it across the whole board.
  • Wordmarks are type, not effects: crisp, set straight, and the split between "Game" and "Unbound" is where the idea lives.

Held fixed: the dark page, the 64px top bar and its 32px mark slot. Cut from round 2: M1 to M8 and W2, W3, W5 and W7. W4 and W6 stay as they were, so the new ones can be judged against them.
