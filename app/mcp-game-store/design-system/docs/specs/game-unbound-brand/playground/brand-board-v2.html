Game Unbound: marks and wordmarks, round 2

Round 1 let pieces float in empty corners of the square, so at 16px the mark became a speck and a dot. Every mark here was drawn to these rules, and each is framed tight to its own outline before it is shown:

  • One compact silhouette that fills the square. Nothing sits apart from the body.
  • Gaps at least 10% of the width, so they survive as a pixel at 16px.
  • At most a handful of masses, one of them orange.
  • Somewhere for motion to go: each card says what would move.

Held fixed: the dark page, the cover green and orange (teal once), the 64px top bar and its 32px mark slot, Bricolage Grotesque. The 16px column is the real favicon raster, blown up 4× so you can see the pixels.

M0 is today's mark. It already passes the rules above, and it can stay if the ball is read as leaving the slot rather than arriving.
