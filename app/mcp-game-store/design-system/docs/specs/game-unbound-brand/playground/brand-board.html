Game Unbound: marks and wordmarks

A whiteboard, not a pick list with an answer. Held fixed: the dark page, the cover green and orange, the 64px top bar, a 32px mark slot left of the wordmark, and Bricolage Grotesque as the heading face (W5 uses its narrow width, the rest its normal width).

Marks are numbered L, wordmarks W. Any mark goes with any wordmark: pick a mark in section 3 and every header and the splash follow it.

1 · Marks

Each at 96, 32 (header) and 16px (favicon), plus a browser tab in dark and light. Every new mark keeps the block shapes and the one orange piece; what changes is the story the piece tells, from "fills the gap" to "got out".

2 · Wordmarks

Each in the desktop bar at 1024px and the signed-out phone bar at 360 and 320px. The readout under each phone bar is the space left once the menu button is placed.
