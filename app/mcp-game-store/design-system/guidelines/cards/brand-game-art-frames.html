Daily Groups

Sixteen equal squares in a 4 by 4 grid on indigo, one colour per row: amber, teal, orange, lilac from the top. The grid stays square and whole; in wider frames it sizes to the height and stays centred.

Casebook

Three upright bars on the bottom edge, orange, lilac and cream, lilac tallest, with a whole amber sun above and to the right. In wider frames the bars narrow and close up; the sun moves right and lower.

36,000 Summers Ago

A lilac peak off the bottom edge, right of centre, an amber sun half behind its left slope, a small teal square low left, a smaller cream square high right, on forest. In wider frames the peak narrows and moves towards the centre.
