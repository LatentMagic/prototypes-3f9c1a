Connect via MCP

Last revised · 23 September 2026

Connect Circlists to your AI assistant over MCP (Model Context Protocol), and it can look through your circles and add to them when you ask. It acts as you, so it only reaches the circles you're in.

Before you start

You need a Circlists account and an AI assistant, such as Claude or ChatGPT. Not all assistants support connecting via MCP.

Connect your assistant

  1. Copy this address: https://api.circlists.com/api/mcp
  2. In your assistant's settings, add a connector and paste the address.
  3. Sign in to Circlists and approve.
  4. Turn Circlists on in a new chat.

Each assistant's own guide shows where the setting is:

  • Claude
  • Claude Code
  • ChatGPT
  • Codex
  • Cursor
  • OpenCode

You sign in with your Circlists account. Your password never reaches the assistant.

What your assistant can do

Once connected, you can ask it to:

  • List your circles
  • Tell you what has arrived
  • Show what's still unread
  • Open an item in full
  • Share an article, video or podcast to a circle, with a thought of your own

It can't reach a circle nobody is paying for right now.

Disconnecting

Remove Circlists in your assistant's settings, where you added it.

Help

If something doesn't work, write to support@circlists.com and tell us which assistant you're using.
