# Changelog

## 2026-10-16. How the site mentions the Pass
- The Pass is named only where something is sold, as "the Pass".
- Pass-only games carry a Pass mark on their picture; Free and First chips are gone.
- Signing in lands on Library; New for you holds only what you can play now.
- A game's Library page has its own card for a free first part and for a player whose Pass has ended.

Landed as a candidate build first; the candidate stays on the record, switched off.

## 2026-10-15. Version 1, the baseline
- Eight games, each with a product page and a library page: Daily Word, Daily Groups, Daily Mystery, Escape, Casebook, Delve, The Barrow of Hollowmere and 36,000 Summers Ago. Only Daily Word and Daily Mystery have real content. The others use sample content.
- Signed in, the app has three sections: Discover, Library and History. Every play is a session with a result, a full record and a share card. Library pages show streaks, achievements and every edition.
- Accounts: sign in by email, Google or Apple, choose a username, connect your AI. Account holds the Pass, notifications, a copy of your data, and deleting the account.
- One paid plan, the Pass, at £30 a year or £3 a month, paid from the first day with no trial. Without the Pass you get the current editions of the four puzzle games and the first edition of Casebook, Delve and the Barrow. The free-and-Pass rules are as proposed on 2026-10-09 and not yet ratified.
