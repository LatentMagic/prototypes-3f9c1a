# Discourse v4 — the set, widened and steered

Working doc. v1's eight directions kept as **directions**, steered by the review
notes, plus three new ones the notes point at. Continuation ideated *inside* each
one. Nothing merged into a rule. Theory lives here; the rig carries a name and
one line.

Built rig: `discourse-playground-v4.html` (+ `pg-d4-*.jsx`).

Two things that table is doing deliberately:

- **The contributor's thought up front is explored, not ruled in.** Five directions
  show it before the read, three hold it back, two seal it until the Swell.
  The tension is visible in the set instead of settled by a paragraph.
- **Ten different typographic treatments of contributed content.** That *is* the
  exploration. One generic line under the attribution is what failed twice.

## Direction by direction

### 00 — Reaction only (baseline)
Unchanged, on purpose. No words anywhere, on either tab. The reference you
compare everything against, and the only entry with no answer to continuation.

### 01 — The invitation *(new)*
**Steer.** Both reviewers wanted the contributor's thought available up front. The
honest version of that note is not "add a line to the card" — it is *the line is
why you would pick this*. So invert the card: the sharer's sentence is the largest
type on it and the article title drops to a source-and-title footnote line.
**Content.** 17px / medium, full measure, name under it in 12.5px semibold. The
headline slot of the card belongs to a member, not to a publisher.
**Continuation.** Your line is *living* (K1): you may rewrite it any time, and it
locks the moment somebody points at it. The record never grows, it ripens.
**Reflections** land behind the door — the card never carries the conversation.
**Cost.** The feed stops being scannable by headline. A circle of eight shares a
day becomes eight paragraphs.

### 02 — Passing notes
**Steer.** Kept as v1 had it, with the preface pulled forward onto Active. The fix
is to the *shape of the record*: v1 rendered a flat stack, so an exchange did not
read as an exchange.
**Content.** A **paired ledger** — thought and the note answering it bracketed by
one hairline into a single unit, pairs stacked with air between them. You can see
at a glance that this is two turns, not a thread.
**Continuation.** One note back **to a person** (K2). Addressed, marked *to Priya*
in a mono eyebrow, rendered beside — never nested. Depth is 1 by construction and
breadth is capped by the size of the circle.
**Cost.** Every read opens an empty slot with your name on it.

### 03 — Marginalia
**Steer.** Jonny liked notes on cards; the user liked the annotations. The v1
version put them *inside* the column, which made them content. Real marginalia
sits **outside** the measure: a narrow right-hand column at width, a left-ruled
gutter below 560px. Notes are 12.5px with initials, never names — the margin is
not a place you perform.
**Continuation.** Unbounded and unaddressed. A margin has no turns because it is
not a conversation — you may add another note in a year. Nothing is ever a reply.
**Cost.** Nobody is answered. Belonging stays ambient.

### 04 — The question
**Steer.** Jonny: the contributor reads as higher priority because they own it and
the response is a reply. Keep exactly that typographic instinct and push it — the
question at 19px / 600, answers at 14px indented under a hairline, so the shape of
a legal exchange (one head, N replies) is visible without a rule.
**Continuation.** Every turn after your first must be a **question** (K4). The
record reads as an enquiry and the floor is always handed on.
**Cost.** A grammar, so it inherits the presumptuousness risk. Sometimes you just
want to agree.

### 05 — Same *(the Echo, made obvious)*
**Steer.** Jonny found it complementary and very interesting; the user did not
understand it. So: the plainer word by default (**Same**, with *Echo* on a lever),
and it appears in **five** places instead of one — under the sharer's line on the
card, under any response, in the record, at the moment of reading, and as the
one-tap answer in place of a composer.
**Content.** No prose in the response position at all. Faces tuck under the
sentence they agreed with, with at most one word beside them. "Priya, Dev and Lena
said the same" is a belonging signal a glyph cannot carry, because it is
*addressed*.
**Continuation.** Pointing is unlimited, and terminal by construction — a *same*
cannot itself be *samed*. Depth stops at one without a rule saying so.
**Cost.** One word cannot hold a disagreement.

### 06 — The vanishing prompt *(new — the strong guided statement)*
**Steer.** The fence Jonny sat on is real, and the two sides are separable: the
*scaffold* helps, the *authorship* stings. This is the version where nothing of
the app's words survives — a rotating prompt in the placeholder, gone the moment
you type.
**Content.** **Epigraphs.** Sentence first, attribution after an em dash, stacked
with generous air. No avatar column, no labels, nothing but member sentences. It
is the same mechanism as 07 and it screenshots nothing like it — which is the
whole point of shipping both.
**Continuation.** Say anything, as often as you like, until somebody writes the
**takeaway** that heads the set (K6). The set's final form is a conclusion, not a
transcript.
**Cost.** The blank box comes back for anyone the placeholder does not reach, and
the middle can sprawl before anyone lands it.

### 07 — Guided statements *(the weak one, kept so the difference can be seen)*
**Steer.** v1's grammar, unchanged and unrescued: the stem is structural and
renders forever. On the **back of the card** — the flip is kept, because the
reviewers liked it and deleting liked work is how you lose a review.
**Content.** Label-led. `Shared because` in semibold, your completion trailing in
regular. This is the version that reads as a form somebody filled in. Put it next
to 06 and the cost is visible in one look instead of one paragraph.
**Continuation.** Restate — pick a new stem and replace your statement. One
current statement per member, always.
**Cost.** The app's words are in the record forever.

### 08 — Inside the door *(pushed hardest — "sick, could be a good grounding")*
**Steer.** The feed and both queues stay completely silent; the card gains
nothing, on either tab. That is this direction's answer to the Read-tab bloat
note, and only this direction's. Then everything the reviewers liked is loaded
into the door:
- **Reactions and reflections as one artefact.** The disc is the *shape* of how it
  landed; the rows under it are the *substance* — a member's **glyph stands where
  their avatar would**, their sentence beside it. Nothing new invented; the
  roster grew the thing it was always missing.
- **The five-second timeout is gone.** The reveal stops being a timed glimpse and
  becomes the record: it opens when you commit, it carries your composer, and
  **you** dismiss it. (Mechanically: the shipped flow is unmounted at commit and
  the record takes the sheet's place — no timer anywhere.)
- **A micro dot** on the door when the record holds words you have not seen. No
  count, no badge.
**Continuation.** Answer **one person** inside the record (addressed, depth 1),
and a new round opens as more of the circle reads it (K5, triggered by reading —
never by a clock).
**Cost.** Discourse you cannot see from the feed is discourse most members never
go looking for.

### 09 — The Swell speaks *(ideator seed 3, un-merged from 08)*
**Steer.** v1 folded the Woven Signal into the door and lost it. On its own it is
the only direction where discourse is **momentary**: you write into the reaction
moment, the words surface *from the disc*, and the card keeps nothing.
**Content.** Words as **labels on the constellation** — each short line pinned at
its author's glyph, radiating off the disc. Reading the record is reading a
diagram, not a list.
**Continuation.** None, deliberately. The moment is the whole of it; the disc
keeps the shape and the words fade. The one arm of the set that says a closed
loop does not need a second turn.
**Cost.** An exchange nobody can refer back to. Also the hardest to read at 390px.

### 10 — The Table *(rebuilt on the user's correction)*
**Steer.** v1 graduated a card the moment anybody spoke, so everything graduated
and the queue split for nothing — the detachment Jonny felt. Rebuilt:
- **Nothing moves out of the feed.** The card stays exactly where it was and gains
  one quiet mono line, *at the table*. The Table is a **lens** over the same
  library, never a second one.
- **Graduation is a member's act**, at two voices or more. Nothing detected.
- **The Table is the only place a conversation may continue** — that is its whole
  justification, and without it the user is right that it is actively harmful.
- **It drains.** Any member may **land it** with what the circle took; that heads
  the record forever and the item leaves the room.
**Content.** Set as a **page** — attribution in the margin, paragraphs in the
column, 19px lead. No bubbles, no per-line times, nothing that reads as a
transcript.
**Cost.** A place you enter is a place you can forget to enter.

## Levers, kept small and secondary

v1 had nine and the reviewer had to configure to find the variety. v4 pre-seeds
every direction so its distinctive moment is one or two touches away, and keeps
six levers folded away for A/B'ing one thing across all of them: **the sharer's
line** (up front / held — the tension, A/B-able everywhere), **names**, **the
word** (Same / Echo), **length**, **thoughts** (as seeded / none / long /
pre-read), **responses** (as seeded / nobody / everyone). Plus Viewport.

## Deliberately not done

- No spine, no rule promoted out of a note, no theory in the reviewer's path.
- No option that varies only turn discipline. K0–K6 are used as *disciplines*
  inside directions, never as entries.
- Nothing applied to `app/`.
