# The returns bar: arrival, removal, and what it says

Delta of 2026-08-26. Built into the main app (`app/talk-return.jsx`,
`app/talk-qa.jsx`, `app/talk-data.jsx`, the LM-652 CSS block in both root
entries). Ratified items are not restated here — this note records only the calls
that were mine to make and the small reads that followed from the ratified rules.

## The removal (mine to decide)

The arrival is the slot opening its own height while the strip travels up into it
— `circ-rise` itself, the New pill's grammar, 560ms on `--ease-quiet`.

The removal is that same motion reversed, at 400ms. Two alternatives were
considered and dropped: a fade in place leaves the space open and then snaps it
shut, which is the "moves twice" the delta forbids; a collapse with no travel
reads as the layout correcting itself rather than the bar leaving. The reverse
gives the displacement back under the same curve, so the feed makes one move in
each direction. It is shorter because leaving is not an announcement — it does
not earn the arrival's full time.

## Staging (mine to decide)

Two rows at the head of the existing `Conversation (LM-652)` section in the
config panel, above `The mark` — the bar is the head of the feed, and the rows
below it are card-level. Labels: **The returns bar** → `Bring it in` /
`Take it away`; **One more card** → `Add a watched card`. The second hint says
outright that nothing changes on screen while the bar is open, so the hold is not
read as a broken control.

## Reads that follow from the ratified rules

- **A row count of one** reads `1 conversation you are watching`. The plural is
  grammatical, not a second copy decision.
- **An emptying held open.** A refresh that empties an expanded bar is held with
  everything else and plays its removal when the member collapses it. Follows
  from the ratified hold; nothing marks it.
- **Changing circle is not an arrival.** Moving between circles takes the new
  circle's state as it stands, unanimated, and collapses the bar. Arrival and
  removal belong to the bar coming and going in front of the member, not to
  navigation.
- **Name order** (ratified 2026-08-26). Names read in the order spoken and must
  not reorder as new turns land: the head line is a sentence, not a ranking, and
  a line read at a glance should not reshuffle. Newest-first governs the bar's
  rows only.
