# Where the work is

Not ratified. This is the last session's reading of what follows from the grid,
handed over for the owner to accept, amend or shred.

## What is spent

**The shape question.** Thirty-odd directions across seven rounds. Everything
that scaffolded what a member says — question, stem, quote, seat, match — died on
one sentence: *"contributors are serious people who have serious thoughts to
share."* The directions still standing are three views of one thing. Generating
more shapes returns to the same family.

## The floor the survivors describe

Stated so it is not re-derived. **Not a proposal, and explicitly not the next
thing to build** — building a single baseline direction was already rejected:

- a line the contributor may attach when adding, **in the existing add popover**;
- a line any member may leave when marking read, **inside the existing reaction
  modal**, skippable;
- the Reaction door holding the record — glyph, spoken depth, line, merged;
- the contributor's note visible before the read; everything said after, behind
  it.

The owner described this himself, without arguing for it: *"that just works,
again with some problems to resolve."*

## The two live briefs

Both are areas he refused to outlaw. Neither has **ever** been designed against a
constraint — the Table was vague, the Dispatch had a cadence, the Stream was a
river. Each broke a law by accident rather than by intent.

### 1. A place that cannot wait and cannot grow

What does a place for conversation look like when **L1** forbids it from waiting
on anybody, and **L4** forbids it from growing without bound? It cannot be a
periodical, it cannot be an inbox, and it cannot be a tab that accumulates. It
also has to answer the return problem: how do you get back to something you have
read, when there is no sort, no filter and no pin, and the card you care about is
three pages down?

Constraints already stated by the owner: three rows beside Active and Read
*"doesn't work very well"*; a configurable wall of cards is *"nonsense… feature
bloat"*; saved cards are *"more of a band-aid"* and should not be assumed.

### 2. A talk surface with a floor and a ceiling

The Stream failed for being unfinishable. A bounded talk surface has not been
drawn. What is a circle's talk when it **cannot** be an endless feed — when it is
finite by construction, has a bottom of the page, and does not become a second
attention centre competing with the queue?

The hard invariant it must survive: **not WhatsApp, not Slack.**

## "Ship nothing" — a genuine contender

Put up deliberately, because until now everything has competed against an
imaginary better yes.

**For:** the circle already has The Swell, which is a real communal signal and is
shipped. The talk members want probably already happens in a group chat they
have, which does continuation better than any bounded mechanism will. Seven
rounds finding no shape anyone believes in is evidence about the problem, not
only about the rounds.

**Against, in his own words:** *"a reaction says something landed but does not
close the loop."* If the talk lives elsewhere, the pulse lives elsewhere.

**Last session's reading:** it loses, but narrowly, and only because of the note
— which is not discourse, it is context on a card, survives every law and every
preference, and he liked it immediately. *"Ship the note and stop"* is a
defensible place to land and would not be a defeat.

## Questions that were withdrawn

Asked, then withdrawn as the wrong register for this stage — recorded so they are
not re-asked cold. They are research questions about need, not design questions:
whether he has actually *felt* reader 2 missing reader 10's thought or whether it
is a completeness instinct; where his circle's talk about a link happens today;
whether the missing magic is in being heard or in hearing.
