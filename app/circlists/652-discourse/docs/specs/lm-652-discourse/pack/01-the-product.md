# Circlists — what it is

## The product

A **shared multiplayer reading queue**. The atomic unit is a URL.

Someone drops a link into the circle → it appears in everyone's feed → each
person reads it externally → each marks it read in their own state. A **communal
library with individual read-state**.

Two tabs: **Active** and **Read**.

## Who it is for

Small trusted circles — a few people who already share links and want one calm
shared place for them. One member (the **champion**) funds the circle; everyone
they invite reads free. Circles stay small by design.

## The Swell — the shipped reaction mechanism

Marking an item read does not just file it away. You leave the circle a
**reaction**: a glyph, and how hard it landed — *a little · moderately · deeply*
— given on a drag pad. Later, anyone who has read the item can open the
**Reaction door** on the Read-tab card and see how the whole circle responded.

The Swell ships and is not being redesigned. **Any discourse idea must state how
it relates to the reaction gesture** — merged with it, adjacent to it, or
sequential after it. Discourse and reactions cannot be two separate systems.

## Feel

- **Confident release** — giving a link to the circle.
- **Quiet cohesion** — receiving what the circle shares.
- **Composed relief** — clearing what is read.

**Calm is the floor.** Anxiety, performance pressure and FOMO are avoided by
design.

## Invariants — settled, not up for discussion

- **Reveal-on-read** — another member's thought, reaction or response on an item
  is not revealed to you until you have read the item yourself.
- **No comment threads.** Threaded open-ended discussion is deliberately parked.
- **No unread badges, no counts, no kudos tallies, no "who read it" signals.**
- **Communal library, individual read-state.** Delete is everyone-delete, a
  shared moderation action, never a private dismissal.
- **Sharing before reading is valid** — "sharing because I trust this writer" is
  a legitimate register and is never punished.
- **URLs only.** No files, no non-link items.
- **Not WhatsApp, not Slack.** Discourse here is centred in what Circlists is —
  a reading circle — not a messaging feature transplanted in.
- **Brevity must be structural** — built into the shape, never policed by rules.

## Voice and visual language

Direct, present-tense, verb-led. Evergreen (no first-visit or temporal framing).
Calm and non-blaming at failures. **No emoji, ever.**

Accent green `#047857` for primary actions, active states and focus rings only —
never status, never decoration. Destructive red `#991b1b` for destructive
treatment only. Hierarchy through size and weight, never colour. 4px grid.
Readable from 320px. Type is Inter, with JetBrains Mono as a mono accent.
