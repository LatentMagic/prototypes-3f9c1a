# The laws — ratified 2026-08-11

Drawn from the owner's own verdicts in `03-the-reactions-verbatim.md`, put to him
individually, and ratified one at a time.

**A law eliminates a direction on contact. A preference argues; it does not kill.**

## The four laws

| | Law | Where it came from |
|---|---|---|
| **L1** | **Nothing waits on other people.** No unlocking on elapsed time, on everyone having read, or on a publishing cadence. | The Seal, The Dispatch — *"we cannot expect people to come back"* |
| **L2** | **Nobody is excluded from speaking.** No cap on who may enter a conversation. | Seats — *"That's not inclusive. This is an inclusive app"* |
| **L3** | **Nothing written is overwritten or replaced.** | Palimpsest — *"that's not fair to the contributor at all"* |
| **L4** | **Nothing grows without bound.** Words per member, per card and per surface are finite by construction, not policed by rules. | The Record — *"it's already too big for the screen… it's going to get bigger and bigger"* |

The invariants in `01-the-product.md` carry in as given and were not re-asked.

## Preferences — they argue, they do not kill

- **P1 — No scaffolding what a member says.** No prompts, sentence stems,
  mandated questions, pulled quotes, matching. *"Contributors are serious people
  who have serious thoughts to share."* This objection alone accounts for the
  owner rejecting seven of the thirteen v7 directions — it is the strongest
  preference in the corpus and should be treated as near-fatal in practice.
- **P2 — No second screen.** The attached thought belongs in the existing
  add-link popover; the reflection belongs in the existing reaction modal.
  Nothing gets a screen of its own. Raised against five separate directions.
- **P3 — The contributor's note may be public before the read.** A permission,
  not a prohibition. Both v1 reviewers asked for it and it has never been built
  properly. Everything said *after* reading stays behind the read.

## Design philosophy — explicitly not a law

**A feature must remove more than it adds.** Belonging to the circle should mean
staying on the pulse *without feeling overwhelmed*. The owner was clear this is
philosophy, not law: *"it's not so much a law."*

## The two areas deliberately left open

Both were put up as laws and **rejected**, in his words because they are
*"half baked but interesting"* and **need more grounded ideation**:

1. **A new place alongside Active and Read.** Attempted three times (the Table, a
   dispatch shelf, an after-note surface). None convinced. Not killed.
2. **A permanently open talk surface.** *"I'm not entirely against that."* Not
   killed.

Treat this as a standing instruction: the laws are not permitted to settle these
two. They are the live work.
