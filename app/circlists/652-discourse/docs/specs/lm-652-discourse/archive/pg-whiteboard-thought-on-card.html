Thought on a card — ten ideations

Ten places a contributor's thought can live inside the shipped card, and ten claims about what a thought is. Static renders only: nothing here is interactive, and no option changes anything outside the card's border.

Every option is rendered as a run of the Active tab: real card, real feed width, the Active tab's own controls — mark as read, then delete. Three cards each, held constant so only the treatment varies: an 80-character thought over a preview, a thought at the 220-character bound with no preview, and a card nobody attached a thought to, because most of the shelf looks like that. Narrow the window for the app-posture read, where the space is scarcest.

On Read the same slot sits beside the Reaction door instead of the check. Nothing here changes anything outside the card's border.

Numbers are how we refer to these out loud. Say which ones to take further, and which pair up.
