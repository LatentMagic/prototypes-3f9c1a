The funding card's third marker word

The card carries a textual marker, never a colour or a symbol alone. Two states are settled: Active and Ending. The third — a renewal whose payment has failed and is being retried — currently says Retrying, which was written, not chosen. This is the choice.

Two tests. Family: Active and Ending both describe the state of the funding; a word that describes what our billing system is doing belongs to a different kind. Division of labour: the marker names the state, the line beneath names the remedy and the stake — if the marker already says the payment failed, the line should not say it again.

In the card — each word with the line it implies

A

Retrying

What ships today.

For. Calm and non-blaming by construction — it says the system is working on it, not that you failed to pay. It is also literally true: the provider retries.

Against. It reassures at the exact moment the line asks you to act. And it is the only marker in the set describing an activity rather than a state, so Active / Ending / Retrying is three states in name and two in kind.

B

Card declined

The marker takes the fact; the line keeps only the remedy.

For. It blames the card, not the member, so it stays non-blaming without being evasive. It is a state, so the family holds. And it lets the line shed its first sentence — the marker says what happened, the line says what to do and what is at stake, one job each.

Against. Two words where the other two are one, and it names the mechanism a little more closely than the voice usually allows.

D

Failing

The same grammatical kind as Ending.

For. Present-tense and continuous, exactly like Ending — the two unhealthy states then rhyme, and the difference between them is legible at a glance.

Against. The loudest word in the set on the calmest surface in the product, and it describes a decline without naming a cause, which reads worse than the situation is.

E

At risk

The marker names the stake instead of the cause.

For. It is about the circle rather than the billing, which is what the member actually cares about, and it needs no knowledge of how payment works.

Against. Risk is anxiety language, and calm is the floor. It is also vague enough that the line has to do all the work — the marker adds nothing you would not have read anyway.
