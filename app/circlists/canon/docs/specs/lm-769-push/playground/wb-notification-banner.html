PWA notifications · reference

The notification banner

One push per circle, one live at a time. The title is the whole design — no body, no actions, no badge count; tap opens the circle’s Active tab. Everything else in the banner belongs to the operating system.

Real pixel sizes, on a neutral ground standing in for whatever is behind.

What we pass

  • title — “New links in Tea Club”.
  • tag: "circle-<id>" with renotify: false — a second link updates the banner already there, silently. This is what makes one-per-circle true.
  • data.url — the circle’s Active tab. Tap is the only affordance.
  • icon, badge — Chromium only, never a dependency.
  • No body, no actions, no counts.
