# Discourse pack — start here

A portable brief for a fresh agent. Everything needed is in these seven files;
nothing outside the pack is required.

**The product:** Circlists — a calm, communal reading queue for small trusted
circles. See `01-the-product.md`.

**The open question:** what shape does discourse take in Circlists — from a
contributor's attached thought to a reader's response — without the app becoming
a chat tool.

**Where we are.** Seven rounds of exploration have produced ~30 built directions
and no answer. The product owner reviewed the latest set (thirteen directions)
and rejected almost all of it. Rather than generate a thirty-first direction, the
last session turned his verdicts into **four ratified laws** and ran every
direction through them. Eleven died. What survived collapsed into a single
family, and two areas were deliberately kept open for fresh ideation.

## The one-paragraph state

Four laws are ratified: nothing waits on other people · nobody is excluded from
speaking · nothing written is overwritten or replaced · nothing grows without
bound. Applied to all thirty directions they kill eleven, and every kill is
unboundedness, deferral, exclusion or destruction. The survivors are all the same
thing wearing different clothes — one bounded utterance per member per item, set
down somewhere that already exists. That is the floor, and it was never the hard
part. The hard part is the two things the owner explicitly refused to outlaw
because they are *"half baked but interesting"*: **a new place alongside Active
and Read**, and **a permanently open talk surface**. Neither has ever been
designed against a real constraint.

## What NOT to do

- **Do not build a playground.** Building another rig from this position was
  rejected outright: too diffuse.
- **Do not generate another set of thirteen directions.** The shape question is
  spent; more shapes return to the same family.
- **Do not decide anything.** See `07-working-with-me.md` — this is strict.
- **Do not promote a preference into a rule that all options obey.** That single
  move destroyed two whole rounds. See `02-the-history.md`.
