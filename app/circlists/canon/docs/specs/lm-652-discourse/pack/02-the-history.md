# Seven rounds — what happened

## The original brief (still the brief)

> What shape does discourse take in Circlists — the full loop, from a
> contributor's attached thought to a consumer's response — so that a circle
> feels on the pulse together without the app becoming a chat tool?

Real testing surfaced the gap: as a contributor you want to attach a thought to
what you share; as a reader you want to respond. A reaction says something landed
but does not close the loop. What closes it is belonging — collaborative, on the
pulse together. That feeling is the magic of the product and it is currently
missing.

The loop has **four layers**, and every proposal is judged across all of them:

1. **Attach** — the thought a contributor adds when sharing a link.
2. **Land** — how that thought reaches a reader (respecting reveal-on-read).
3. **Respond** — the reflection a reader gives back.
4. **Continue** — whether a conversation can carry on, and how.

## The rounds

- **v1 — eight structurally distinct directions.** Two reviewers gave notes *on
  individual options*. Liked: inside the door ("sick"), the Echo, the card flip,
  reactions and reflections shown together, and — from both reviewers — the
  contributor's thought being visible **before** you read.
- **v2 / v3 — rejected.** The v1 notes were read as a verdict rather than as
  notes. One preference was promoted into a global rule, six of the eight
  directions were demoted to "levers", and the set collapsed into one mechanism
  wearing twelve names. Owner's words: *"the same fuckin' note that's occurring
  on a card… but there's an emoji in a different place."*
- **v4 — ten directions,** the variety restored, continuation ideated inside each
  one. Built; never reviewed in writing.
- **v5 / v6** — no surviving record.
- **v7 — thirteen directions.** Reviewed in full. This is what
  `03-the-reactions-verbatim.md` covers, and it is the most valuable document in
  the pack.

## The failures that are still binding

From the v2/v3 postmortem. These are rules, not history:

- **Notes on options are notes on options.** Extrapolating a note should
  *multiply* directions, never merge them. If an ideation pass ends with fewer
  distinct surfaces than it started with, it has gone wrong — say so and stop.
- **Never promote a preference into a global rule mid-exploration.** A rule every
  option obeys is a rule that can no longer be seen, compared or rejected.
- **A new dimension is a new axis, not a replacement question.** Continuation
  should have been ideated *within* the existing directions, not instead of them.
- **Variety must be visible.** If two options screenshot the same, they are one
  option.
- **Design the content, not just the plumbing.** Any new content class on a card
  needs real typographic treatment, and different directions should treat it
  differently — that *is* the exploration.

## Two failures specific to v7

Worth knowing before proposing anything, because they poisoned the review:

- **Unreachable affordances.** Several directions had a "Continue" button that
  went nowhere, so the continuation layer was judged by clicking dead controls.
  We do not actually know whether those continuations are bad or merely absent.
- **The rig was buggy** — state resetting mid-play, a double-add, the Reaction
  door losing its disc. Defects were indistinguishable from design failures, so
  part of the review is a review of bugs.

## Nothing has shipped

No discourse work has ever been applied to the product. The prototype is
untouched.
