# Prompt as received (2026-08-14)

Verbatim brief for the v11 playground: "Circlists — the thought on a card, and
how it opens". Six options for one question — where an attached thought lives on
the card, and how it opens and puts away — three committing to the MARGIN and
three to TUCKED UNDER, framed in the real Active feed with the length control as
the decisive lever. The brief's settled list, named fail states, levers, seed
copy and playground shape are the binding constraints; see the session record.

Key clauses carried into the build, so they are not re-derived:

- Nothing above the title. No quotation-mark framing. The preview thumbnail
  stays. The card face is not where conversation sprawls. The thought is not
  scaffolded. The thought reads as a person's words. Nothing grows without bound.
- Fail states: a silent collapse · universal truncation · losing the reader's
  place · the freckled shelf · uneven rhythm.
- Give the margin the same effort as tucked-under: it has never been shown with
  an interaction at all.
- Config: thought length (none / one line / a paragraph / a paragraph and a few
  bullets) is the most decision-relevant control; preview present or absent;
  density one-in-four versus every card. Both viewports, 390px included.
- The reveal must actually work — clickable, animated, playable.
