# Reference

* [Circlists Brand — Assets](README.md) - The shippable Circlists brand assets — the SVG marks, their PNG exports, and the app-icon set, and what each is for.

# Subdirectories

* [icons](icons/index.md) - The Circlists favicon and installed-icon set — the mark composed on a ground, at the sizes the browser and OS dictate.
* [lockup](lockup/index.md) - The Circlists lockup (mark + wordmark) — ink and cream SVGs, and their transparent PNG exports.
* [mark](mark/index.md) - The Circlists mark — the canonical SVG and its transparent square PNG exports.
* [motion](motion/index.md) - The Circlists mark in motion — three animated treatments (pulse, spinner, micro), and the motion each carries.
* [native](native/index.md) - The Circlists native app's icon sources for Android and iOS — the Capacitor inputs plus the themed, dark and tinted layers the tool does not make.
* [wordmark](wordmark/index.md) - The Circlists wordmark — ink and cream SVGs, and their transparent PNG exports.
