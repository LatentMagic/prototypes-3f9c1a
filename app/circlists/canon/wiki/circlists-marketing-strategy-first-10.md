# Circlists — Marketing Strategy (first 10 customers)

> [Circlists](circlists.md)'s go-to-market strategy for its first **10 paying customers** (~£30/mo) — 10 champions, each paying £3/space. Strategy only; execution follows agreement.

## In one line

Win 10 lovable circles, one at a time, by hand — and let lovability do the recruiting.

## The two phases

This strategy spans two releases:

- **Alpha** — friends & family only; hyper-personal and support-driven. Deliberately below a complete v1 — the lovable core is there, the finish isn't. It's where we land our first real users and earliest leads, and get the first honest signal on whether it's lovable enough to spread.
- **v1 / full release** — the public, complete, self-serve product. Here the channel motion opens to strangers — cold outreach, community, launch surfaces.

The rest of the doc sequences against these: the **warm spine seeds the alpha**; the **wider channels fire at v1**.

## Diagnosis — the real problem

The crux is **not reach**. Circles are everywhere and we have many channels.

The hard thing: every sale hinges on one person — the **champion** — making a bundled, upfront bet:

> pay £3 · set the space up · pull ~9 people into a new shared habit — *before* the payoff (a living shared space) exists.

£3 is a low cost but real money — not nothing — and it multiplies for a champion running more than one circle. That multi-circle stickiness is part of why £3 (down from £9): the ask stays light per-circle while revenue still scales with usage. Alongside the money, the bet still carries real weight in effort and social capital — doing the setup, and asking ~9 people to join something new.

So the binding constraint is **converting + activating that one champion**, not generating awareness.

A few lovable early wins compound: an activated circle retains, spreads by word-of-mouth, and the 10-cap turns members into champions of their own circles. The leverage is the **quality of the first activations**, not the volume of outreach.

## Who we're winning

- **10 customers = 10 living circles** (small networks), not 10 individuals. Build tiny complete circles one at a time.
- **The champion is the hard side** — does the work and pays the £3/space. Invitees are free (the easy, sticky side). All acquisition targets the champion.
- **Five circle types, all in scope, none ranked:** engineering pods · book clubs · study cohorts · market-news circles · close friends & couples.
- **Targeting heuristic (not a gate):** weight effort toward champions who already lead a live group and like to curate — whether by role (eng managers, book-group admins, cohort leads) or by passion (the enthusiast a niche defers to, hub of the group without a formal title) — but message broadly, warm and cold.

## Positioning — the why

- **Enemy:** links **dying in the group chat** (friends) / **scattered across Slack channels and DMs** (teams).
- **What we are:** the calm, shared place a circle's reading lives. A *shared* reading space — not a solo read-later app.
- **One emotional core, wording flexes by audience** — calm = relief from overwhelm + nothing gets lost:
  - Individuals feel *overwhelm* → calm, own-pace.
  - Teams feel *scatter* → one place, nothing lost, aligned. (A team never gets "no rush" language.)

**Copy (locked):**

- **Core** — "The calm, shared place your circle's links live — instead of dying in the group chat."
- **Friend** — "Drop in what's worth reading — everyone reads at their own pace, no pressure, nothing gets lost."
- **Team** — "Your team's reading in one calm place — not scattered across Slack channels and DMs. Nothing lost, everyone on the same page."

## Guiding policy — the bet

**Win one lovable circle at a time, by hand — and let lovability do the recruiting.**

- **Win champions one at a time, by hand** — concentrate effort on reaching each champion personally and making the bet feel worth taking, not on broad awareness.
- **Lovability is the growth engine** — make the first activations so good they spread; the 10-cap + word-of-mouth compound the few into more.
- **The marketing engine and the company's moat are the same thing** — lovability here = our [Simple, Lovable, Complete](../../company/identity.md) conviction lived out (taste + polished craft).

> ⚠️ **Honest dependency:** the policy leans on lovability to spread — yet the alpha is deliberately **sub-SLC**, not the simple, lovable, complete product. That's what v1 (and beyond) is for. We're betting the alpha is lovable *enough* to start the compounding; carried as a named assumption, not a given.

## Coherent action — what we do

Proximate objective: **10 champions as a spread** — no single route carries all 10.

Channels sit on a **value × effort** axis — core · cheap-win · stretch. Nothing valuable is cut; stretch = opt-in or timed.

One principle runs across every channel:

- **The homepage carries the why** — outreach and posts are short, human doorways; the public [homepage](circlists-homepage.md) (visual, self-explaining) does the selling. A **build-log** ("what's new" on the homepage) keeps it visibly alive — proof it's not abandonware, the trust-check every channel routes through (and serves as foundation for SEO treadmill).

### Core

**1 · Outreach — the spine.** *(everything leans on this)*

Reaching champions by hand, one at a time, in two registers. Prefer one-to-one over a group chat where it fits — a personal ask lands harder, and it mirrors the product's own pitch: things shouldn't die in the group chat.

- **Warm** — our own network (friends, family, a few colleagues). We approach them personally and out of the blue — an honest support ask about their reading life, not a pitch → drop the homepage link, relaxed, no chase. With friends & family the ask can be plainly supportive — *"set up a space, give it a go, support us."* These first activations are the highest-leverage thing we do.
- **Cold** — champions and companies we don't know; opens at full release. Partly deliberate, partly catching someone naming the pain (a "links lost in our Slack" post) → short, contextual note → the homepage link → no chase. Channel flexes: company / team leaders via LinkedIn DM or work email (UK law allows cold B2B email — the cleaner target); organisers in promo-protected spaces via direct platform DM, where cold-emailing a personal address isn't allowed.

**2 · Community participation.**

Be a genuine participant where the circles gather — Reddit, Discord, Goodreads groups, Slack, Silent Book Club chapters. Reddit is richest.

The line isn't comment-vs-post — it's **narrative in a sanctioned slot** (Showoff Saturday, Show HN, self-promo threads, maker subs: "here's why I built it") vs **a bare ad in a protected feed** (pulled). Menu to work it: cold narrative posts · sanctioned slots · read each space's rules first · give-first replies · mine recent pain-posts and reply.

Some spaces bar promo outright — respect their T&Cs and reach the organiser directly instead.

### Cheap-win

**3 · Launch directories.** AlternativeTo · SaaSHub · Smol Launch · Web Curios · Launching Next.

Quick, low-effort, submit-and-forget, permanent. **Presence, not acquisition** — they catch the person actively searching "alternative to Slack for sharing links," plus search/AI visibility. We do them because they're near-free, and bank nothing on them. Position as "alternative to Slack link-sharing / shared folders"; the 10-cap is an anti-noise *feature*, not a limit.

**4 · Tell our own networks.** Post the build announcement — *"we built this"* — to our own personal walls (LinkedIn etc.). Quick, free, reaches warm contacts at scale. A one-off broadcast, not a sustained posting cadence (that's Stretch).

**5 · Reserve social handles.** Grab the product handles now — quick, free, worth doing even if we never post.

### Stretch — later, to extend reach *(opt-in or timed — deferred, not cut)*

None of these is the first-10 effort; that stays deliberately personal. These extend reach *after*, as time and the product allow.

- **Free first month (v1 lever)** — a v1 exploration with Jonny: comp a first month to lower the £3 cold barrier and reach beyond friends & family. Paddle coupons give us this capability. *Anchor — in progress:* part of a wider set of upcoming features, including more in-app upselling.
- **Public launch & listing surfaces** — post the product to the bigger public boards for a visibility bump; opt-in, timed, near-free — we bank nothing on them. *(e.g. Show HN, Product Hunt, G2/Capterra.)*
- **Automated / agent marketing** — a Hermes-style agent extending reach later. Not for the first 10 — that needs the personal touch.
- **SEO** — a longer game we don't skip: the homepage build-log compounds into search reach over time. Builds beyond the 10, not a first-10 lever.
- **Sustained social posting** — a regular cadence on the product's handles or a founder's own account, if we choose to later; optional, never an obligation, no follower-chasing grind. (Distinct from reserving the handles — a cheap-win above — and from one-off announcements / LinkedIn outreach.)

## What we deliberately skip (the no-list)

Genuine *nevers* — distinct from the Stretch items above, which are "not yet."

- **No paid, full stop** — ads, newsletters, classifieds, any spend. No budget, and it buys awareness, not the champion's leap.
- **No enterprise / org-wide selling** — v1 can't serve a company-wide, IT-led rollout (no admin/SSO, 10-cap). A single *team* inside a company stays in scope — that's just a champion's circle; what's out is the admin-tier deployment.
- **No building features just to chase a channel** — no contorting the product for a marketing surface (e.g. concierge / done-for-you setup).

## Assumptions & open risks

- **Lovability** — the whole policy rests on the product being lovable enough; still maturing. It grows gradually toward an SLC release, steered by our analytics and customer emails. *(named above)*
- **Product name — resolved to Circlists** (2026-07-07). The *message* is unaffected; "space" / "circle" wording and reserved handles revisit under the settled name. ("Pulse" left the name but survives in the copy where it earns its place.)
