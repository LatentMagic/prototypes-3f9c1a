# Circlists — Copy Voice

The rules that steer how Circlists copy is written — so future copy holds the voice without re-deriving it each time. Sits alongside the [brand system](brand/circlists-brand.md) and the audience messaging in the [marketing strategy](circlists-marketing-strategy-first-10.md). Product: [circlists.md](circlists.md).

## Rules

- **No pressure / anti-FOMO.** Never "keep up" / "catch up" — they imply you're behind. Calm is the floor.
- **Format-neutral.** What's shared spans articles, videos, podcasts — never narrow to "read" ONLY. Name the formats or stay neutral.
- **Name the content, not the mechanism.** Avoid "link" if possible — it's the plumbing, and it breaks the moment we go beyond links. Name what people actually consume; more durable, and stronger for search.
- **Active, not fed-to-you.** The circle contributes; it is not an algorithm curating for you. No passive / "curated-for-you" framing.
- **Async, not synchronous.** Own-pace, private per-person state. Avoid "together" where possible — it implies same-time, which the product isn't.
- **"Pulse" = the world's ever-changing current** (finger-on-the-pulse) — not a person, not the champion.
- **"Circle", never "space".** The group is a circle. "Space" is not our word.
- **"List" over "queue".** Both read fine and "queue" is not wrong — but the name carries "list", so it wins. Locked homepage copy leans this way already: "Stay on the pulse with your circle... only your list changes". The name, [Circlists](circlists.md), carries both halves.
- **Champion lens (a perspective, not a fixed phrasing).** [Champions](circlists-marketing-strategy-first-10.md) are the ones who pay and lead — worth holding their point of view when writing copy, though it won't always show up as literal phrasing.

## Copy that works

Lines that hold the rules. Reach for these before writing new — each earns its place by what it manages to avoid.

- **"Shared list, your own place in it"** — the whole superposed-state promise, in six words. Avoids "private", which elsewhere on the site means *confidential* and here would mean *individual* — one word, two jobs. Avoids "read", which narrows the format. Echoes [positioning](circlists-positioning.md)'s "each member keeps their own place".
- **"Drop a link. Everyone sees it. Read, watch or listen at your own pace — only your list changes."** — names all three formats rather than defaulting to reading; "at your own pace" carries the calm floor; "only your list changes" states superposed state without naming the mechanic.
- **"Stay on the pulse with your circle."** — "pulse" as the world's current, the circle as the group.
