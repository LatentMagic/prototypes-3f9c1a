# Changelog

## 2026-10-29. Every move, the same for every game
- Every game's Every move list is the design system's move list, closed, below the game's own section.
- Guess, Daily Mystery, Delve, Escape, Chain and Where write their lines in the same grammar: the label names the line, the first part says what the move was in the game's own words, the rest qualify it.
- Chain and Where have session states of their own: in progress, an earlier play that failed, and an earlier play.

## 2026-10-29. Delve's session page, told in its own terms
- Delve's session page shows the hero's character sheet, then the run: the result statement (the ending word and its line), the route of rooms with every roll as a die, and the held roll beside it.
- On a phone the sheet opens folded to the hero, hit points and threat.
- The route carries down to Delve's now card on the library game page, a step smaller; each die opens the session page on that roll.

## 2026-10-28. Session pages told in the game's own terms: Escape and Daily Word
- A session page now has two parts: a shared top section, the same for every game, and below it the play told in the game's own terms.
- Escape's session page shows the rooms and locks you came through. Daily Word's shows the board, with the hint row and the result statement.
- Each game's result statement carries down to its now card on the library game page: Escape's open door, and Daily Word's last guess beside a six-row trail.

## 2026-10-20. The product is named After Pixels
- The placeholder `[Platform]` and the working name MCP Games are now After Pixels everywhere in the app, including the Pass ("the After Pixels Pass") and the two directory listings.
- Links and contact addresses are on afterpixels.com.

## 2026-10-18. Notifications and the data copy removed
- Notifications, the push banner and "copy of your data" are gone from the app (descoped by the owner, 2026-10-16).

## 2026-10-16. How the site mentions the Pass
- The Pass is named only where something is sold, as "the Pass".
- Pass-only games carry a Pass mark on their picture; Free and First chips are gone.
- Signing in lands on Library; New for you holds only what you can play now.
- A game's Library page has its own card for a free first part and for a player whose Pass has ended.
- Ten more games, all Pass-only.

Landed as a candidate build first; the candidate stays on the record, switched off.

## 2026-10-15. Version 1, the baseline
- Eight games, each with a product page and a library page: Daily Word, Daily Groups, Daily Mystery, Escape, Casebook, Delve, The Barrow of Hollowmere and 36,000 Summers Ago. Only Daily Word and Daily Mystery have real content. The others use sample content.
- Signed in, the app has three sections: Discover, Library and History. Every play is a session with a result, a full record and a share card. Library pages show streaks, achievements and every edition.
- Accounts: sign in by email, Google or Apple, choose a username, connect your AI. Account holds the Pass, notifications, a copy of your data, and deleting the account.
- One paid plan, the Pass, at £30 a year or £3 a month, paid from the first day with no trial. Without the Pass you get the current editions of the four puzzle games and the first edition of Casebook, Delve and the Barrow. The free-and-Pass rules are as proposed on 2026-10-09 and not yet ratified.
