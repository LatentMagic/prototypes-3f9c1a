Round 2: two different ways to say how the run ended

Round 1, below, was one idea laid out four ways. These two change where the result lives (A) or what form it takes (B). Each shows desktop on the left and one phone per ending on the right. The rolls and longest streak are hidden; the Tweaks panel's "Show the tally" puts them back where each concept would carry them. delve.md keeps both in the play's record and puts both on the share card; it doesn't ask the session page to show them.

A

The result is the roll box

No row above the route. The box on the right, which today shows a roll when you pick one, shows the result when nothing is picked: the word, what happened, and the roll it ended on. Picking a roll swaps the box to that roll; picking it again, or leaving, brings the result back. On a phone the box leads and the route follows. Cost: the result is out of view while a roll is open.

B

Status strip

The result stops being a heading and becomes the card's edge: one filled strip across the top, in the ending's status colour with its dark on-colour, the word and what happened on one line. The tone is carried by the fill, so the word can be smaller and the strip is only 48px tall. In progress is a quiet neutral strip. Cost: the word is no longer the biggest thing on the card, and colour does work that size did before.
