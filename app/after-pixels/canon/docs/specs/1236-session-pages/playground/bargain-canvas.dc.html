The bargain's cost: symbols for "the final foe is tougher"

Sea salt now sits in Abilities, so the Bargain group says only what the deal costs. Each idea is shown struck and not struck: a bargain is optional, so the group needs an empty state. The label is always "Final foe"; the figure carries the +1, +2 or +3 (tweak the cost). The glyphs are drawn like the shop's icons: 1.75 stroke, square caps, mitred joins.

Delve sheet: showing a bargain

A bargain gives the hero something now (here an ability, Sea salt: one use, heals 4) and costs something later. When the hero reaches the final foe, the foe's track starts that many steps further along (1, 2 or 3 in today's editions). Each option is the right-hand end of 3B's band at full size. Tweaks: the cost, and whether the player's view sends the number (today it only sends a sentence).
