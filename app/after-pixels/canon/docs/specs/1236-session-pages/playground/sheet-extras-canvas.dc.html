Delve character sheet: where the extras go

Low fidelity. The extras are the strong-hit streak, clues, the light, a bargain struck, and an ability the run grants. Each appears only when it applies. The sheet's band is full, so each concept puts them somewhere other than the band: under it (A), on the route (B), in the roll box (C), or on the sheet's second face (D). The Tweaks panel switches between no extras, the streak alone, and all of them. The torch, the sea witch's bargain and Sea salt are made up for this canvas.

1A

A second band

The sheet grows a row under a hairline, headed "This run" where the hero sits above it. Same cell anatomy as the band. With no extras the row is gone and the sheet is exactly as today. A granted ability sits here with its bargain, not in Abilities. On a phone it is one more row inside M1's fold. Cost: the sheet gets taller by about 90px whenever anything applies.

1B

On the route

The extras are things that happened, so the route draws them where they happened. The light burns down under each room's name. A clue and a bargain mark the room they came from. A streak is a line beside its run of dice, which is also the open "streak on the route" item. The sheet keeps its band; only Abilities grows, by the granted ability (dashed). Cost: the run's state now is read off the route's last column. With the granted ability the band only fits by squeezing (tighter padding, names on two lines), as shown; the real band would need the same.

1C

In the roll box

The box on the right already tells one roll. It gains a "When rolled" block: the streak, clues, light and bargain as they stood at that roll. At rest it shows the latest roll, so it is also the run as it stands. Picking an earlier die shows the run back then. The box has height to spare beside a tall route. Granted ability joins Abilities, as in B. Cost: the extras are only seen one roll at a time, never as a whole.

1D

The sheet's second face

The band keeps its size and gains a back. Under the hero's class, a switch reads "Hero · This run", shown only when something applies. "This run" swaps every group after the hero for the extras, in the same band. Shown here: both faces. On a phone it is A, because M1's fold already hides the rest. Cost: on the default face the extras are a tap away, and the hero cell grows a control.
