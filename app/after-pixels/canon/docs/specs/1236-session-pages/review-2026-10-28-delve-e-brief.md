## Character sheet (his brief, verbatim)

> A new concept for Delve's session page: a character sheet, showing the hero and how their run stood. It holds:
>
> - The hero's name and class
> - Hit points left, of their maximum
> - Threat level reached, of its maximum
> - Equipment carried, out of the carry limit (two or three by setting; three in The Goblin Warren)
> - Abilities, with uses left
> - Inspiration: has it or not
> - The six stats: Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma (each a bonus added to the d20)
>
> Keep it light and elegant, a sheet you glance at, beside the path and the key moments. Use the space well on desktop and phone.
