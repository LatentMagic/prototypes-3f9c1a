# Motion art for the other four games (draft, not ratified)

Written in the system's format (`guidelines/game-art-motion.md`). Same rules: 400ms, 50ms stagger in, edges anchored, shapes whole, nothing scales the tile. Built in the prototype (`GS_ART_LOCAL`, `.ga-*` in `index.html`) until the system takes them. Each is drawn on a centred 4:3 stage, so square and wide frames show the whole piece with more ground, never a crop.

### Daily Word
Three tiles in a row on forest: amber and cream solid, a teal outlined tile on the right.
- Always: tiles are equal squares; the outline tile is hollow.
- Hover: amber and cream round into dots in a wave; the outlined tile rounds and steps up and right.

### Daily Mystery
A magnifier. Orange ring on wine, a cream bar to the right with an amber cap.
- Always: ring whole; the cap sits on the top of the bar.
- Hover: the ring turns square; bar and cap round their tops and rise together, 50ms behind.

### Escape
A locked door. Teal door on indigo, runs off the bottom edge, amber handle, cream window top left.
- Always: door touches the bottom edge; handle sits on the door.
- Hover: the door's top rounds into an arch; the handle turns square and steps up; the window rounds and steps up and right.

### Delve
A doorway into the warren. Lilac arch on wine, indigo opening, three amber steps, a torch either side.
- Always: arch runs off the bottom edge; steps stay inside the opening; flames sit on their posts.
- Hover: the opening grows 7% from its base; posts grow a quarter taller and the flames ride up and turn square.
