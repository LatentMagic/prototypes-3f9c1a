Motion

Two treatments, both the design system's own: the loader and the hover. Each falls back to the still mark under reduced motion. Pulse and micro are not made: After Pixels has no context for them yet.

A 1.2 s cycle on the design system's one curve. One slot at a time turns orange, becomes a circle and lifts out, then fades and returns, the four squares offset round the mark. Calm, not fast: a load that ends early should end the loader, not hurry it. With reduced motion the loader is the resting mark.

Hover or focus the mark or the lockup. The circle lifts further out, two squares draw in 6 px and the whole mark tilts 8° anticlockwise: 440 ms in, 330 ms back. It leaves the 120 box on purpose, so the file's viewBox is padded (154 square) and the mark draws smaller in it than the plain mark. Only where hover exists; under reduced motion it stays still. In <img> it cannot receive hover: use <object> or inline it.

Colour

Every value is the design system's, from tokens/colors.css. Strong colour appears only in the mark, covers, shape blocks and status. Ruled out: blue anywhere, navy as a ground, a player-chosen colour. A hex button copies it.
