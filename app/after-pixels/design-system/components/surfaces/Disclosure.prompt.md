Disclosure is a heading that opens and closes a short block of text; use it for "How it plays" and similar optional detail.

```jsx
<Disclosure title="How it plays">Claude deals the puzzle, you answer in the chat, and the game scores it.</Disclosure>
```

It can also hold a list: a session page's MoveList, titled with the list's title. The list runs to the Disclosure's edges, with a line above its first row, and keeps its rows' own colours; the muted body text is only for a block of text.
