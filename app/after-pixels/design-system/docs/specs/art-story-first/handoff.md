# Game art: story first, then motion

Decided 2026-10-19, ratified by the owner.

## Why
A review of a cover's hover found the end pose was never designed, pixel-capped moves fell short on large covers, the meaning was written after the move was picked, the worked examples had become the only vocabulary, and nobody checked frames partway through the motion.

## What changed in `guidelines/game-art-motion.md`
- New section **Story first, then motion**: the rest stands on its own, the end pose is the payoff, and the change itself tells the story. Each piece carries one line: "At rest, X. On hover it becomes Y, by Z."
- **Motion**: any move is open, rotation included. The "loader's own moves" wording is gone. Borrowing another piece's move is flagged as a warning sign.
- Rule 3 is now **Land at any size**: a move that has to meet something is set by where it ends; only open-ended moves keep the pixel cap.
- New section **Review in frames**: rest, halfway and end at the smallest and largest sizes, plus a five-question checklist.
- "Say something about the game" is folded into the story section; Daily Mystery and Daily Word stay as worked examples.
