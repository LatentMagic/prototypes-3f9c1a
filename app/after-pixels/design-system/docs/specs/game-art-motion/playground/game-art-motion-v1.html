Game art: motion and shape

Three motion directions on the same two pieces. Hover the art or the title; both run the same hover. Nothing scales up, so the layout never shifts.

A

Loader beat

The loader's own move: each shape in turn rounds into a circle, hops up and to the right, dims and settles. Plays once per hover, about 1.2s end to end.

B

Depth

The shapes sit on layers at different distances and follow the pointer at different rates. The ground stays put and the sun drifts the other way, so it reads as far behind.

C

Play

Each game does a small gesture of its own, looping gently while hovered. Daily Groups locks one row at a time. Casebook's line-up shifts on its feet under a setting sun. On leave it eases back to rest.

One description, any frame

Each piece is specified in words first. The words say what must stay true and what may move, so the art can be recomposed for square, landscape or wide slots without losing anything.

Daily Groups

The four groups of a solved board. Sixteen equal squares in a 4 by 4 grid on indigo, one colour per row: amber, teal, orange, lilac from the top.

Always
The grid is square and whole. Gaps are about a third of a square. At least one gap's width of indigo on every side.
May change
In wider frames the grid sizes to the height and stays centred; indigo fills the sides.

Casebook

A line-up under a low sun. Three upright bars stand on the bottom edge, orange, lilac and cream, with an amber sun above and to the right of them. Indigo ground.

Always
Bars run off the bottom edge. Lilac is tallest, cream shortest. The sun is a whole circle and never touches an edge or a bar.
May change
In wider frames the bars get narrower and closer together, and the sun moves further right and lower relative to the frame.
