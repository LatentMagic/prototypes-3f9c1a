# Product-page cover: title over light shapes

## Problem
On the product page the label and title sit on the cover in light text. Where a light shape (cream) runs under the title, the text fails AA. Dark text is not an option on the dark grounds.

## Decision (2026-10-19, ratified by the owner in chat)
Written in `guidelines/game-art-motion.md`, "Title on the cover (product page)".
- Light shapes stay in the right half of the drawing.
- Test every cover with its real label and title at every product-page width. No text touches a shape it fails AA against.
- A fail is fixed by redrawing the art. No text change, no scrim, no plate.
- A new or renamed game is tested again.
- The product page is the only surface with text on art; art that passes there needs no check elsewhere.
