# Logo concepts (from Gemini, gemini-3.8-flash-high)

1. **The Gambit** — A solid square split along the diagonal, with the upper piece advancing forward into open ground to make the opening move. 16px: two chunky solid masses separated by a 3px diagonal channel that stays sharp.
2. **The Dialogue** — Two solid semicircular voices staggered across a divide: the prompt leaning in from upper left, the counter-move answering from lower right. 16px: two chunky curved silhouettes separated by a clean 4px diagonal clearance.
3. **The Threshold** — A heavy portal framing a clean negative-space doorway: the entrance to the escape room, vault or crime scene. 16px: a narrow aperture framed by solid square pilasters and an arch, unmistakable on any background.
4. **The Deduction** — A grid of three squares where the player supplies the missing piece: a warm circle slotted into the open quadrant. 16px: a 2x2 cluster of blocks split by a clean cross channel, keeping the circle distinct from the squares.
5. **The Phase** — A circular dial tracking the daily cycle: a half arc anchored below, today's quarter lit, tomorrow's slot waiting. 16px: a solid circular silhouette with a bold quadrant notch, no fine lines.
